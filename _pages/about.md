<section id="about" data-reveal>
  <div class="wrap">
    <div class="sec-head"><span class="idx">01</span><h2>About</h2></div>
    <p class="statement">I've never found a clean line between being a <span class="muted">researcher</span> and being an <span class="muted">engineer</span>. The problems worth working on need both at once — the instinct to chase what matters, and the rigor to make it hold up in the real world. That overlap is where I do my best work.</p>
    <div class="prose">
      <p>I'm a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at <a href="https://www.tesla.com/AI">Tesla</a>. My work centers on <strong>agent systems</strong> — infrastructure, orchestration platform, harness, memory, apps, and benchmarks — along with <strong>enterprise ontology</strong>, <strong>multimodal recommendation</strong>, <strong>vision perception</strong>, and <strong>distributed systems</strong>. My current focus is building superintelligence (SI)–driven manufacturing and service solutions for Tesla's vehicles and Optimus. Along the way, I've been fortunate to be recognized as one of Tesla's Most Impactful People (2024) and as Top Talent in 2025 and 2026.</p>
    </div>
    <p style="color:var(--accent-soft);font-family:var(--sans);font-size:.95rem;font-weight:600;letter-spacing:0;margin:34px 0 10px">Interests &amp; research focus</p>
    <ul class="interests">
      <li><strong>Agentic AI Infra &amp; Platform</strong> — the full stack that makes agents dependable: harnesses for tool use and control, long-horizon memory, agent-native apps, visual orchestration at scale, and enterprise ontology for live data, knowledge, and memory.</li>
      <li><strong>Evaluation &amp; benchmarking</strong> — rigorous, reproducible benchmarks and verifiers for agents and AI systems, measuring whether skills, tools, and reasoning actually hold up across diverse, open-ended tasks.</li>
      <li><strong>At-scale human &amp; persona simulation</strong> — for now a digital, online medium: modeling human behavior and personas at scale for full-environment personalized user testing, scientific research, and even social policy and decision-making — and, looking even further, a key to how embodied, physical AI will one day personalize its interaction with people.</li>
      <li><strong>ML systems</strong> — full-stack, production-grade ML across NLP, LLMs, vision perception, multimodal recommendation, and causal inference — from infrastructure and pipelines to monitoring and the full model lifecycle.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="experience" data-reveal>
  <div class="wrap">
    <div class="sec-head"><span class="idx">02</span><h2>Experience</h2></div>
    <div class="prose">
      <p>My path runs through AI &amp; manufacturing, media &amp; telecom, and venture capital — most recently at <a href="https://www.tesla.com/AI">Tesla</a>, where I engineer <strong>enterprise AI and agentic ecosystems</strong> from <em>first principles</em>, turning frontier research into AI products that ship under real, high-stakes constraints.</p>
      <p>At <a href="https://www.comcast.com/"><strong>Comcast</strong></a>, I was in an enterprise ML/DS rotation delivering applied machine learning and data science across a product's entire surface — growth, UX, finance, and workforce — reading a product <em>360°</em> through a DS/ML lens. At the venture firm <a href="https://www.alphaedison.com/"><strong>Alpha Edison</strong></a>, I built an <strong>AI-driven decision-making</strong> engine for investing, with a sharp instinct for locking onto the real pain points and opportunities, and for <em>where value is actually created</em>.</p>
      <p>I take a class of concrete problems and abstract them into a research problem to solve — a way of working I developed in research at USC's <a href="https://www.isi.edu/centers-ckg/">Information Sciences Institute</a> on the DARPA LORELEI <a href="https://usc-isi-i2.github.io/thor/">THOR Project</a> (knowledge graphs, entity resolution, online clustering), later applied in <a href="https://www.defense.gov/">U.S. DoD</a> settings, working alongside <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=U1A6iBMAAAAJ&hl=en">Pedro Szekely</a> and <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=fetkEu4AAAAJ&hl=en">Mayank Kejriwal</a>. I hold an M.S. in Applied Data Science from the <a href="https://www.usc.edu/">University of Southern California</a>.</p>
      <p>The through-line is a rare, end-to-end fluency across the full arc — from locking onto a pain point or opportunity, through research and first-principles engineering, to a product running in production.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
